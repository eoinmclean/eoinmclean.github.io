Three Nouns: Field Notes

Insect • School • Cattle

A small site about systems: tiny coordination, structured learning, and managed biology.

Concept

What ties these nouns together?

Insects demonstrate decentralized coordination and adaptation. Schools formalize knowledge and behavior through rules, schedules, and culture. Cattle represent domesticated ecosystems shaped by human goals: food systems, labor, and land use.

Together, they point to a shared theme: systems of control and cooperation across nature and institutions—how groups move, learn, and survive under constraints.

Required list

  • Insect: swarm logic, niches, resilience
  • School: structure, identity, social learning
  • Cattle: domestication, agriculture, economics
